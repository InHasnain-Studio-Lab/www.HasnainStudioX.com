What every policy has in common

  • No account, sign-in or registration is required to use the software.
  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking is built into any application.
  • Files you open, create or export stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • AI features run locally on your own CPU or GPU, no prompts or images are sent to a server.
  • Store licensing and payment are handled entirely by Microsoft or Google; the studio never sees your payment details.
