Privacy Policy

ConvertMaster Ultra is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Every conversion runs on your device.

Effective date: 30 July 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: ConvertMaster Ultra for Android (com.convertmaster.app), version 0.3.0

1. Overview

ConvertMaster Ultra converts and compresses video, audio, image and PDF files entirely on your device, using an on‑device media engine.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not upload your media to any server.
  • We operate no server that receives your files or your usage.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.
  • The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash reporting SDK.

All conversion happens locally. Files you pick are read locally and results are written locally.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

ConvertMaster Ultra does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or account. The app has no sign in.

Usage Data or Analytics

No screen views, session lengths, feature counters, or behavioural events.

User Content

The videos, audio, images and PDFs you convert are never uploaded, copied off device, or inspected remotely.

Sensitive System Data

No contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS, clipboard monitoring, or installed app list.

Location Data

No location permission is requested and no location is derived.

We run no server of our own, so your media cannot be uploaded even by accident. The only outbound calls the app can make are listed in section 4.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Theme mode, accent, reduced effects, haptics, and job queue concurrency, stored in the app private preferences.

Your Outputs

Files produced by a conversion, plus a local database of job and output records so the Queue and Library can list them.

Local Cache

Temporary working files created during a conversion, held in the app private storage.

Local Licence State

A local flag recording whether the one‑time Pro unlock is active, plus any temporary rewarded unlock window.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Files you explicitly saved or shared elsewhere remain where you put them.

4. Network and Third Party Services

These are the only outbound calls the app can make, and exactly what each one sends.

Purchase Validation

When you buy or restore the Pro unlock, the app sends the product id, the Google Play purchase token, and the app package name to a Hasnain Studio X validation endpoint so the purchase can be verified with Google Play. No media and no personal data are included.

Google Play Billing

Purchases are processed by Google Play. See section 5.

Google AdMob

Builds with advertising enabled show Google AdMob ads, which use the Android Advertising ID and standard ad request data. Ads never receive your media or your file names. The Pro unlock removes advertising.

Outbound Links

Opening the website, this policy, or the contact email hands off to your browser or mail app, which have their own policies.

5. Google Play Purchases

The one‑time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the entire payment flow.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • No card number or billing address ever reaches the app or the developer.
  • We do not receive your Google account information.

The app receives only a purchase token confirming that a purchase occurred, which it uses to unlock Pro features.

6. App Permissions

Media and File Access

Used only to read the files you pick for a conversion and to write the result. The app does not scan or index your library in the background.

Internet and Network State

Used for purchase validation and, in builds with advertising enabled, for ad requests. It is never used to transmit your media.

No permission in this app is used to send your content anywhere. Conversion is entirely local.

7. Children's Privacy

ConvertMaster Ultra is a general audience utility. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to delete on request.

8. Policy Changes

This policy is updated whenever the app data behaviour changes, and the effective date and version at the top are updated with it. The current version corresponds to ConvertMaster Ultra 0.3.0, effective 30 July 2026. Any change will remain consistent with our core principle: your data stays on your device.
