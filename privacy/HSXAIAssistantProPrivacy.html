Privacy Policy

HSX AI Assistant Pro is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Every question, picture and document is processed on your PC.

Effective date: 3 October 2026  ·  Version: 1.0.0.0  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: HSX AI Assistant Pro (Windows App)

1. Overview

HSX AI Assistant Pro is an AI assistant that runs entirely on your PC. Its language and vision model works on your device, so it never needs the internet to answer you.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit your questions, files, pictures or answers to any server.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All AI processing happens locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

HSX AI Assistant Pro does not collect, store remotely, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, account or identifiers. The app has no sign in.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.

Your Conversations and Files

What you type, paste, attach or drop into the app is read by the on-device model only. It is never uploaded.

Sensitive System Data

The app reads your processor, memory and graphics card only to show performance guidance on screen. This is never transmitted.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your theme, accent, assistant style, the optional notes you write about yourself, and engine preferences are saved locally under your user profile only.

Conversation History

If history is on, conversations and the pictures you attached are saved in the app's own data folder so you can return to them. You can turn history off or delete it at any time in Settings.

Local Cache

The on-device model is held in memory while you use it and released afterwards. Files you save from the app go only to the folder you choose.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it. Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

HSX AI Assistant Pro is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Minimum Required

The app declares only the full trust desktop capability, which lets it run its on-device AI engine as part of the app. It requests no camera, microphone, location or contacts access.

File Access

The app opens only the pictures and documents you choose or drop into it, and saves only where you choose.

On-Device Processing

Answers, summaries, text extraction from pictures and document analysis are all produced on your PC by the bundled model.

Network Access

None. The app makes no internet connections and blocks its AI engine from reaching the internet. The Microsoft Store handles the licence check. The website and report links on the About page open in your own browser or mail app, only when you click them.

6. Children's Privacy

HSX AI Assistant Pro is a general-audience productivity app with always-on content safety. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
