Privacy Policy

WorkX Suite is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Everything you open or create stays on your device.

Effective date: 6 October 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: WorkX Suite for Android, version 1.3.0

1. Overview

WorkX Suite is a productivity app for Android with three modules, XPDF, XDOC and XMATH, and a document assistant that works on this device. Everything you open or create stays on your device unless you save it elsewhere or share it yourself.

  • We have no account system and no sign in.
  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell your documents.
  • The app contains no advertising or analytics service of ours. Google's on‑device ML Kit components send Google limited diagnostic data, described in section 4.

All viewing, editing, text recognition, translation and assistant answers happen on the device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

WorkX Suite does not collect, store on a server, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, sign‑up, or account of any kind.

Your Documents

Your PDFs, Word, text, Excel and CSV files never leave the device unless you share or save them yourself.

Location and Contacts

Location and contacts are never requested or accessed.

Camera and Biometrics

The app does not use the camera or biometric sensors.

No server ever receives your documents, so they cannot be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Recent Files

The name, size and last opened time of recent files, in app‑private storage.

Working Copies

WorkX Suite edits its own copy of each file you open, plus any exports you make from it. Closing a file, or removing it from the Files list, deletes its copy.

App Settings and Logs

Theme, reduced effects, language pack settings, and a small local activity log (up to about 2 MB, with file names) used for troubleshooting. The log is never sent anywhere.

Local Licence State

Whether the Pro unlock is active and when it was last confirmed, plus the Google Play purchase token while a purchase is waiting to be confirmed.

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. Earlier versions could store an API key for an online AI provider; that feature is gone and any key left behind is deleted on the next start.

4. Network and Third Party Services

These are the only outbound calls the app can make, and exactly what each one sends.

Purchase Validation

When you buy or restore Pro, and about once a week afterwards to re‑confirm it, the app sends the product id, the Google Play purchase token and the app package name to a Hasnain Studio X validation endpoint, which checks the purchase with Google Play. No documents and no personal data are included.

Language Packs

When you choose to download a language in Settings, Languages, the app shows its size (about 30 MB) and asks first. Google Play services then fetches that language model; the request names the model, never your documents. Downloads can be restricted to Wi‑Fi and removed on the same screen. Translation then runs on the device.

Google ML Kit Diagnostics

Google's ML Kit components for text recognition and translation send Google device and app information, an installation identifier, performance and error details, and the languages used, for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google encrypts it in transit and does not share it with third parties. Your documents and text are not included.

Fonts

The app typeface (Inter) is downloaded from Google Fonts on first use and kept on the device. Exporting a PDF that contains non‑Latin text downloads the matching Noto font from Google Fonts. These requests carry your IP address and standard request headers to Google, never your documents.

Opening the website, this policy or the contact email hands off to your browser or mail app, which have their own policies.

5. Google Play Purchases

Pro is a one‑time, non‑consumable purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles the entire payment flow.

  • We never see or store your card number or billing address.
  • We do not receive your Google account information.
  • Our validator receives only the product id, the purchase token and the package name.

6. App Permissions

Internet

Used for purchase validation, language pack downloads, font downloads and Google's ML Kit diagnostics. Never used to send your documents.

Google Play Billing

Lets Google Play process the Pro purchase.

Files

No storage permission is requested. Files you pick in the Android file picker, or share into WorkX Suite from another app, are opened one at a time, and Save to device writes only to the place you choose.

7. Children's Privacy

WorkX Suite is a general audience productivity app. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Policy Changes

This policy is updated whenever the app's data behaviour changes, and the effective date and version at the top are updated with it. The current version corresponds to WorkX Suite 1.3.0, effective 6 October 2026. Any change will remain consistent with our core principle: your data stays on your device.
