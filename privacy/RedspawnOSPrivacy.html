Privacy Policy

RedspawnOS is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Your desktop shell, wallpapers and widgets all run on your PC.

Effective date: 10 October 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: RedspawnOS 1.0.0 and later (Windows App), formerly HSX RedspawnOS

1. Overview

RedspawnOS is a Windows desktop shell with a dock, widgets, a command palette and live wallpapers, built with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy. It reads your system state and shows it to you on your own screen.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit your system information to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

Everything the app shows and stores stays on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

RedspawnOS does not collect, store, or send any of the following to us:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, account, sign-in or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind. The word “telemetry” in the app only means the wallpaper showing your own PC’s activity on your own screen.

Your Content and Files

Notes, pinned apps, clipboard history, imported wallpapers and screen captures are never uploaded.

System Readings

Processor, graphics, memory, network and battery readings, the list of installed apps and the list of open windows are read only to show them to you on screen. They are never transmitted.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location. Weather uses the coordinates of the city you choose, not where you are.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded to us even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your ink, wallpaper, widget layout, pinned apps, weather city and preferences are saved under your user profile, in the RedspawnOS folder of your local app data.

Your Notes and Wallpapers

Quick notes and any image or video you import are kept in that same folder. Clipboard history is held in memory only and is gone when the app closes.

Screen Captures

A capture is taken only when you ask, and saved to the RedspawnOS folder in your Pictures library.

Error Log

A small log of recovered errors is kept in the app folder to help diagnose problems. It contains no personal content and is never sent anywhere.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes its folder; captures you saved to Pictures stay.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

RedspawnOS is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.
  • The rating button only opens the Microsoft Store rating page. Nothing is offered or withheld in return for a rating.

5. App Permissions

Full Trust Desktop

RedspawnOS runs as a full trust desktop app so it can act as a desktop shell: list and launch your apps, show and switch windows, read hardware counters, control volume and brightness, and lock or sleep the PC when you ask.

Windows Taskbar

The app hides the Windows taskbar and shows its own dock only after you agree, the first time you run it. Desktop mode, the tray icon and Exit bring the Windows taskbar back, and it returns automatically if the app closes.

Start With Windows

Off until you turn it on in Settings. It uses the Windows startup task, which you can also switch off in Windows Settings.

Internet Access

Used for exactly two requests: the Microsoft Store licence check, and the weather forecast for your chosen city, where the coordinates, rounded to four decimals, are sent to MET Norway. Settings lists every request made in the current session. No data is sent to Hasnain Studio X.

Every wallpaper scene is drawn on your PC. No system data is sent off the device.

6. Children's Privacy

RedspawnOS is a general-purpose desktop utility. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
