Privacy Policy

HSX SocialDeck Pro is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Your brands, your drafts and your queue are held on your own PC, and the app never posts anything on your behalf.

Effective date: 6 September 2026  ·  Version: 1.0.0.0  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: HSX SocialDeck Pro (Windows App)

1. Overview

HSX SocialDeck Pro is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps, and your drafts are never sent to us.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.
  • The app never publishes anything for you. You post it yourself, on the platform.

Your brands, drafts, queue and settings are written to a plain file in your own app data folder and stay there.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

HSX SocialDeck Pro does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. The app contains no analytics library of any kind.

Your Passwords

You sign in to each platform on that platform’s own page. The app never sees, stores, or transmits a password, and it has no account of its own.

Your Drafts and Posts

Nothing you write is uploaded to us, retained by us, or used to train anything.

Sensitive System Data

No credentials, no browsing history from outside the app, and no system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers for this app, so nothing can be uploaded to us even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your mode, accent ink, reminder timing and the switches on the Settings page, stored in a small local settings file.

Brands and Brand Voice

The brands you create, their handles, and the tone, emoji, hashtag and call to action rules you set for each one.

Drafts and Queue

Every post you compose, the per channel drafts written from it, and anything you have queued with its due time. All of it lives in one readable JSON file you can open, copy or delete yourself.

Browser Sessions

Each brand gets its own browser profile folder, so signing in under one brand never touches another. The cookies in it belong to the platforms you signed in to, exactly as they would in an ordinary browser. The app does not read, copy, or transmit them, and Settings can clear a brand’s sessions at any time.

Local Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

The Writer

The model that writes your posts is a file installed with the app on this PC. It reads the line you type and your brand voice, writes the posts, and holds nothing afterwards. There is no account, no key to enter, and nothing it reads or writes is transmitted.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export, share, or publish it.

4. Network and Third Party Services

The app makes only the connections listed here. Every one of them is disclosed. Writing your posts is not one of them.

The Platforms You Open

The Hub opens each social platform in an embedded browser container at your request. Those are the platform’s own connections to its own servers, exactly as if you had opened the site in a browser, and they are governed by that platform’s own privacy policy. We are not a party to them and we receive nothing from them.

Searches You Start

When you press “Look it up” on a claim, the app opens a web search for that phrase in the same embedded browser panel. Only the words of the claim you picked are used, and the search happens because you asked for it, exactly as if you had typed it into a browser yourself. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press it, and no search is ever run in the background.

Microsoft Store Licence Check

A licence query to Microsoft when the app starts, to establish whether you are in the trial or own the full version. No content and no personal data is included.

Writing Uses No Network At All

The writer runs entirely on your own processor or graphics adapter. Writing a post, rewriting it, and suggesting hashtags all happen on this PC, with the network switched off if you like. Nothing you type is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Replies and Checks Are Local Too

Text you highlight on a page to draft a reply from, or to see what in it can be checked, is read by the app on this PC and handed to the writer running here. It is never uploaded, never stored beyond the window you are working in, and never seen by us.

No Other Calls

Apart from the two above, and links you deliberately click, the app makes no outbound connection of its own.

5. Microsoft Store Licensing

HSX SocialDeck Pro is a paid app with a free Microsoft Store trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

6. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

The app requests internet access and standard desktop permissions, and nothing else. Permissions are used solely for app features and never for data collection.

File Access

Only its own folder in your app data, where the deck file, the settings and the browser sessions are kept. The app does not scan your documents, your pictures, or your drives.

Clipboard

The app puts a draft on the clipboard when you press Copy or Open composer, so you can paste it into the platform yourself. It does not read your clipboard.

On-Device Processing

Writing a post for each channel, rewriting it, suggesting hashtags, applying your brand voice, and running the queue all happen on your own PC.

7. Children's Privacy

HSX SocialDeck Pro is a general-purpose productivity tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

8. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
