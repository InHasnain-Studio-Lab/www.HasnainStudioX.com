Privacy Policy

Mobile TuneX finds large files, duplicate photos and unused apps, and reads your battery, network and device details. Every scan runs on your phone. There is no account and no cloud, and we never sell or share your data.

Effective date: 6 Oct 2026  ·  Version: 8.3.0  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: Mobile TuneX for Android

1. Overview

  • All scans and analysis run locally on your phone.
  • We have no servers that store your data, and no account is needed.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash reporting service.
  • We do not sell, share or monetise user data.
  • The few network requests the app makes are listed in full in section 4.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

Mobile TuneX does not collect, store on a server, or send us any of the following:

Personal Identity

No name, email, phone number, address or account.

Usage and Analytics

No telemetry, tracking or advertising ID. The app shows no ads.

Your Files and Media

Photos, videos and files are read on your phone only. They are never uploaded.

Installed Apps and Usage

The installed app list and app usage times are read on your phone only and never shared.

Location

No GPS or device location is read.

We have no servers that receive your data.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Settings and History

Your preferences, battery readings history, speed test history and scan times.

Compressed Copies

Compress Media saves smaller copies in your gallery's "Mobile TuneX" folder. Your originals are only deleted if you choose to.

Screen Log

A local list of which screens were opened, kept in the app's private storage. It is never sent anywhere.

Pro Purchase State

A local Pro flag and the last Google Play purchase token, so Pro works offline and can be re-checked.

This data never leaves your device unless you export or share a report yourself. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

4. Network Requests and Third Party Services

Purchase Verification

When you buy or restore Pro, the app sends the Google Play purchase token, the product id and the app's package name to our verification service, which checks the purchase with Google Play. Nothing else is sent, and the token is not stored by us.

Speed Test

A speed test downloads and uploads test data with Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com). The upload is random bytes, never your data. Cloudflare sees your IP address and the app's name, as any website would.

Custom Speed Test Server

If you enter your own https server address, the test downloads from that server only. That server sees your IP address.

Fonts

On first use the app downloads its typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) and keeps them on your phone. Google sees your IP address for that download.

Links You Tap

Links to our website, email or Google Play open only when you tap them, in the app you choose.

All requests use encrypted HTTPS. No advertising, analytics or crash reporting service is included.

5. Google Play Purchases

Pro is a one time purchase handled entirely by Google Play.

  • Card and payment details never reach the app or us.
  • We do not receive your Google account information.
  • On each start the app asks Google Play which purchases this account owns, so a refunded purchase is removed. This check happens between your phone and Google Play.

6. App Permissions

Photos and Videos

Large Files, Gallery Scan and Compress Media. Asked when you first open one of them, after an explanation. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Installed Apps

Apps Scan, Unused Apps, the game list and Notification Manager read the app list after an explanation. It stays on your phone.

Usage Access

Special access you turn on in Android settings, used to show when apps were last used. It stays on your phone.

Uninstall Requests

Opens Android's uninstall dialog for an app you choose. Android asks you to confirm every app.

Do Not Disturb Access

Game Mode only. Your own Do Not Disturb setting is restored when the session ends.

Notifications

Maintenance reminders, only after you turn them on.

Biometrics

App Lock, if you turn it on. Android handles your fingerprint or face; the app only receives yes or no.

Network and Wi-Fi State

Speed test, purchase verification, and the Wi-Fi link speed and signal shown in System Info.

Declining any permission only turns off the feature that needs it.

7. Children's Privacy

Mobile TuneX is a general audience utility. It is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from any user.

8. Policy Changes

This policy applies to Mobile TuneX 8.3.0 and later, effective 6 Oct 2026. When the app's data behaviour changes, we update this page, its version and its effective date in the same release.
