Privacy Policy

HSX TerraOrbitix 3D is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. It has no account, no sign‑in and no tracking of any kind.

Effective date: 25 July 2026  ·  Version: 1.0.0.0  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: HSX TerraOrbitix 3D (Windows App, formerly TerraX Vision Ultra)

1. Overview

HSX TerraOrbitix 3D is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not operate any server, so nothing is ever sent to us.
  • We do not use telemetry, analytics or advertising of any kind.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

The whole-planet basemap and its terrain are built into the app, so HSX TerraOrbitix 3D draws the globe with no internet connection at all. Going online is optional and only adds closer detail. When you do fly in close, the app requests extra map squares from third-party mapping providers; those requests identify only the square being drawn, never you. Section 5 covers this in full.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

HSX TerraOrbitix 3D does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers. The app has no account and no sign-in.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs. The app ships with no third-party analytics libraries of any kind.

Your Places and Routes

Saved places, routes, waypoints and captured images stay on your device. They are never uploaded and we never see them.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Your Real-World Location

HSX TerraOrbitix 3D does not use GPS or any device location service. The coordinates in the app are wherever you chose to look on the globe, not where you are.

We do not run servers for this app, so no data can reach us even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your view preferences, chosen layers, detail level and last position are saved locally on your device only.

Your Saved Work

Places, routes, camera sequences and exported images you create are written to your device and never transmitted.

Map Tile Cache

Downloaded map squares are cached on disk so revisiting a place is fast and uses less data. You can clear this cache at any time from Settings.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

HSX TerraOrbitix 3D is a paid app with a free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

The app asks Windows whether your licence is a trial or a purchase, and reads the answer locally. That check does not identify you to us.

5. Network Access and Map Data Providers

Full disclosure of every outbound request HSX TerraOrbitix 3D makes. There are no others.

Works Offline

The whole-planet basemap and its terrain ship inside the app. With no connection the globe still renders everywhere, and no request of any kind is made. A connection is needed only for street-level detail, never to use the app.

Closer Detail

When you fly closer than the built-in basemap covers, the app requests extra map squares and terrain from third-party mapping data providers. Each request identifies only the square being drawn.

Orbital Data

When you enable satellite tracks, the app requests current orbital reference data from a third-party provider so the tracks are accurate.

What These Requests Contain

A map square reference, or a request for orbital reference data. Nothing else. No account, no identifier, and nothing about you is attached. As with any internet request your IP address is visible to the provider serving it, which is how the internet works and is not something we collect, log or receive.

Never Sent

Your saved places, routes, camera sequences, captured images and settings are never included in any request and never leave your device.

Minimum Permissions Only

HSX TerraOrbitix 3D declares internet access and standard desktop app permissions. It requests no camera, microphone, contacts, or location permission.

File Access

Only when you choose to import or export a file, or save an image. There is no background scanning.

These providers have their own privacy policies covering the requests they receive. We have no agreement with them about you, receive nothing back from them about you, and never send them anything you create in the app.

6. Children's Privacy

HSX TerraOrbitix 3D is a general-audience reference and exploration tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
