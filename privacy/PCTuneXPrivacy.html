Privacy Policy

PC TuneX is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Every diagnostic and repair runs on your PC.

Effective date: 13 July 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: PC TuneX (Windows App)

1. Overview

PC TuneX is a Windows maintenance and repair toolbox built with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy. It reads your system state and runs repairs directly on your PC.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit your system information to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All diagnostics and repairs happen locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

PC TuneX does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.

Your System Information

PC TuneX reads details such as drives, drivers, and processes only to show them to you on screen. This information is never transmitted anywhere.

Sensitive System Data

Recovered Wi-Fi keys and exported reports are shown or saved only on your device, at your request, and never sent to us.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your theme, accent, and safety preferences are saved locally under your user profile only.

Reports You Export

System reports, driver lists, and app inventories are written only to the folder you choose, such as your Desktop.

Local Cache

Temporary working files used while a tool runs, cleared automatically afterward.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

PC TuneX is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Full Trust Desktop

PC TuneX runs as a full trust desktop app so it can perform real maintenance, such as clearing caches, resetting the network stack, and repairing system files. These actions run locally.

Administrator Approval

Repairs that change protected settings ask for Windows administrator approval each time, so you always stay in control.

On-Device Processing

Every scan, cleanup, and repair is performed on your PC. No system data is sent off the device.

Network Access

Used only for the Microsoft Store licence check and for network tools you start yourself, such as Ping, Traceroute, the DNS benchmark, and the connection speed test. Those tools contact the public servers being tested (for example your DNS provider or a public speed-test host), never us. No data is sent to Hasnain Studio X.

6. Children's Privacy

PC TuneX is a general-purpose utility tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
