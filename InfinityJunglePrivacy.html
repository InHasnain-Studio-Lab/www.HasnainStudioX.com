Privacy Policy

Infinity Jungle is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy.

Effective date: 4 July 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: Infinity Jungle (Windows App)

1. Overview

Infinity Jungle is an offline 3D survival game developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy. Its entire world is generated on your PC.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All processing, including world generation, rendering and game logic, happens locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Infinity Jungle does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Gameplay Data

No play sessions, progress, or expedition saves are ever uploaded anywhere.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your mouse, view and sound preferences are saved locally on your device only.

Expedition Save

Your survival progress, journal and story chapter stay on your device and are never transmitted.

Local Error Log

A plain text log written only if something goes wrong, kept locally to help you report an issue by email if you choose to.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

Infinity Jungle is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

Infinity Jungle requests only the minimum permissions required for core functionality. Permissions are used solely for app features and never for data collection.

File Access

The game reads only its own bundled content and its own settings folder - never your documents or personal files.

On-Device Processing

The whole jungle, its sounds and its story are generated and simulated entirely on your PC. Nothing is streamed or computed remotely.

Network Access

Used only for the Microsoft Store licence check - and even then, no data is sent to us.

6. Children's Privacy

Infinity Jungle is a general-audience adventure game with mild fantasy combat. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
