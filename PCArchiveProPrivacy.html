Privacy Policy

PC Archive Pro is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy.

Effective date: 10 August 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: PC Archive Pro (Windows App)

1. Overview

PC Archive Pro is an offline archive workspace developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All processing happens locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

PC Archive Pro does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Your Files and Archives

No archives, files, previews, or search results. Everything you open, extract, create, or encrypt is processed only on your device.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, passphrases, vault contents, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your preferences, theme, recent archives, and search history are saved locally on your device only.

Password Vault and Containers

Vault items are sealed on your device with Windows DPAPI. Encrypted containers you create are ordinary local files protected with AES-256-GCM; only you hold the passphrase.

Local Diagnostics

If something goes wrong, a small error log is written to your local app data folder so problems can be diagnosed. It is never transmitted anywhere.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

PC Archive Pro is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

PC Archive Pro requests only the minimum permissions required for core functionality. Permissions are used solely for app features and never for data collection.

File Access

Only when you choose a file, drop one onto the app, or open an archive from Explorer — never background scanning.

On-Device Processing

Extraction, compression, encryption, hashing, and search all run on your own hardware. Nothing is sent out for processing.

Network Access

The app makes no network calls of its own. The Microsoft Store licence check is performed by Windows; no data is sent to us.

6. Children's Privacy

PC Archive Pro is a general-purpose productivity/utility tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
