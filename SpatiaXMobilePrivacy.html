Privacy Policy

SpatiaX Ultra Mobile — Hasnain Studio X Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 Aug 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: All Hasnain Studio X Windows apps

1. Overview

All Hasnain Studio X apps are designed with a local-first, privacy-first approach.

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • We do not transmit any data to our servers — we do not operate servers.
  • All processing happens locally on your device.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

Your data stays on your device — always.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Hasnain Studio X apps do not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Content You Create

Nothing you create inside the app — prompts, text, images, or files — is uploaded or transmitted.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your preferences and configuration are saved locally on your device only.

User-Created Content

Saved prompts, notes, or any content you create stays on your device.

Local Cache

Temporary performance cache stored locally and never transmitted.

Licence / Trial State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. Google Play Billing

Some apps may offer in-app purchases or one-time unlocks. All billing and transactions are handled exclusively by Google Play.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Google account information.

Google manages everything securely under their policies.

5. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

Hasnain Studio X apps request only the minimum permissions required for core functionality. Permissions are used solely for app features and never for data collection.

File Access

Only when you choose a file — never background scanning.

Clipboard Access

Only when you paste or copy within the app.

Network Access

Only if the app fetches online content for you — and even then, no data is sent to us.

6. Children's Privacy

Our apps are general-purpose productivity and utility tools. They are not directed to children under 13 and do not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
