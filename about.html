Hasnain Butt Akhtar

Founder and sole developer of Hasnain Studio X, an independent software studio publishing local-first applications for Windows and Android. Seventy-six titles to date, every one of them built on a single rule: your files are processed on your machine, and nothing is sent anywhere.

The full list is on the Windows apps and Android apps pages, and the generative tools have their own AI Studio page.

How I build it

Every application I publish follows four rules, and I do not make exceptions for convenience:

  • Local-first. Processing happens on your CPU or GPU. There is no server to send work to, because I do not run one.
  • No telemetry. No analytics SDK, no usage tracking, no crash reporting that ships your data off the device.
  • No accounts. You do not register to use software you have already installed.
  • No subscriptions. A tool you paid for keeps working. Licensing is handled by the Microsoft Store or Google Play, so I never see your payment details.

Each application has its own published privacy policy stating exactly what it does and does not touch. All of them are listed on the privacy policies page.
