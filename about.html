What Hasnain Studio X® Is

Hasnain Studio X® is built on a single engineering principle: your data stays on your device.

All applications run fully on‑device, on PC or mobile, with no cloud processing, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, no accounts and no subscriptions. Users retain complete control over their data, and nothing is transmitted externally.

This principle guides every product decision: how features operate, how files are handled, and how privacy is protected.

Engineering Principles

Hasnain Studio X® operates under four core rules.

Local‑first

All processing occurs on the user's PC or mobile device. No external servers are used.

No telemetry

No analytics SDKs, usage tracking, or off‑device crash reporting.

No accounts

Software does not require registration or online identity.

No subscriptions

One purchase provides lifetime use. Licensing is handled by the store platforms.

Each application includes a published privacy policy detailing exactly what it does, and does not, access. All of them are listed on the privacy policies page.
