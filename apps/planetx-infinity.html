PlanetX: Infinity

Explore an entire solar system, simulated locally

A solar system explorer for Windows. Orbits, rendering, physics, voice callouts and progression are all computed locally on your PC — photo mode saves images straight to your own Pictures folder, and the only network use is an optional live weather layer.

What PlanetX: Infinity does

  • Full solar system simulation with orbits and physics
  • Photo mode saves directly to your own Pictures folder
  • All computation runs locally on your PC
  • Optional live weather layer — no accounts, no identifiers sent

Who it is for

PlanetX: Infinity is built for anyone who enjoys exploring simulated worlds without an always-on connection.

It sits in the games & explore part of the Hasnain Studio X catalogue, and it follows the same rule as every other title in the range: the work happens on your own hardware. Nothing is uploaded for processing, because there is no server to upload it to.

Local-first, by design

Most software in this category sends your files somewhere to be handled. PlanetX: Infinity does not. Processing runs on your CPU or GPU, and your files stay in the folders you put them in. PlanetX: Infinity does reach the internet for the content it displays, but nothing about you or your files travels the other way.

That is not a setting you enable. It is how the application is built, and it is why there is no account to create, no profile to build and no subscription to lapse. Full detail is in the PlanetX: Infinity privacy policy.

Questions

Does PlanetX: Infinity need an internet connection?

Yes, because PlanetX: Infinity works with content that lives online. That connection is used to fetch that content and nothing else: your settings, your history and your files are held on your device and are never sent to Hasnain Studio X.

Does PlanetX: Infinity require an account?

No. There is no registration, no sign-in and no online identity. You install the application and use it.

What data does PlanetX: Infinity collect?

None. There is no analytics SDK, no usage tracking and no crash reporting that leaves your device. The full detail is in the PlanetX: Infinity privacy policy.

Is PlanetX: Infinity a subscription?

No. PlanetX: Infinity is a free trial followed by a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There is no recurring fee.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
