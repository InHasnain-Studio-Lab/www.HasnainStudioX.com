Who it is for

SpatiaX Ultra is built for editors, musicians and anyone who works with sound or footage and does not want it uploaded for processing.

It sits in the audio & video part of the catalogue, and your audio files never leave the machine to be processed. There is no server behind SpatiaX Ultra to send them to.

Local-first, by design

Plenty of the alternatives send your audio files to a server to be handled. SpatiaX Ultra does not. Processing runs on your CPU or GPU, and the tracks you play stay where you put them. The application keeps working with the network switched off.

That is not a setting you enable, it is how SpatiaX Ultra is built, which is why there is no account to create and no subscription to lapse. Full detail is in the SpatiaX Ultra privacy policy.

Questions

Does SpatiaX Ultra need an internet connection?

No. SpatiaX Ultra does its work on your own device. You can install it, disconnect, and it keeps functioning. A connection is only used by the store itself, for installation and licence checks.

Does SpatiaX Ultra require an account?

No. There is no registration, no sign-in and no online identity. You install the application and use it.

What data does SpatiaX Ultra collect?

None. There is no analytics, no usage tracking and no crash reporting that leaves your device, and your audio files are never uploaded. The full detail is in the SpatiaX Ultra privacy policy.

Is SpatiaX Ultra a subscription?

No. SpatiaX Ultra is a free trial followed by a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There is no recurring fee.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
