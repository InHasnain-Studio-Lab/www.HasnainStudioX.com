HSX EarthShell

Turn your desktop into a live map of the world

HSX EarthShell replaces your Windows desktop with a living map. Pin your home place, keep your taskbar and windows, and fly anywhere on Earth as your wallpaper - map tiles you have already seen are cached locally so it keeps working offline.

What HSX EarthShell does

  • Live world map as your desktop shell
  • Home place, saved layout and offline tile cache
  • Taskbar, windows and clipboard history stay on-device
  • No account, no sign-in, no telemetry

Who it is for

HSX EarthShell is built for people who want their machine to run properly without handing a cleanup tool the keys to their data.

It sits in the system & performance part of the Hasnain Studio X catalogue, and it follows the same rule as every other title in the range: the work happens on your own hardware. Nothing is uploaded for processing, because there is no server to upload it to.

Local-first, by design

Most software in this category sends your files somewhere to be handled. HSX EarthShell does not. Processing runs on your CPU or GPU, and your files stay in the folders you put them in. HSX EarthShell does reach the internet for the content it displays, but nothing about you or your files travels the other way.

That is not a setting you enable. It is how the application is built, and it is why there is no account to create, no profile to build and no subscription to lapse. Full detail is in the HSX EarthShell privacy policy.

Questions

Does HSX EarthShell need an internet connection?

Yes, because HSX EarthShell works with content that lives online. That connection is used to fetch that content and nothing else: your settings, your history and your files are held on your device and are never sent to Hasnain Studio X.

Does HSX EarthShell require an account?

No. There is no registration, no sign-in and no online identity. You install the application and use it.

What data does HSX EarthShell collect?

None. There is no analytics SDK, no usage tracking and no crash reporting that leaves your device. The full detail is in the HSX EarthShell privacy policy.

Is HSX EarthShell a subscription?

No. HSX EarthShell is a free trial followed by a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There is no recurring fee.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
