HSX NanoCodify

Private code and creation studio for Windows

A code and creation studio that works inside the folder you open on your own PC. Every session, every file change and every extension you build is written as a plain file you can read, edit or delete yourself. No account, no telemetry, nothing reported back.

What HSX NanoCodify does

  • Works directly in the folder you open on your own machine
  • Every session saved as a plain readable file you own
  • Line-by-line record of every change made to your files
  • Build your own agents, instruction packs and slash commands
  • Hooks and automation triggers you define yourself
  • No account, no analytics, no crash reporting

Who it is for

HSX NanoCodify is built for developers who want their source, their history and their tooling to stay on their own machine.

It sits in the developer tools part of the Hasnain Studio X catalogue, and it follows the same rule as every other title in the range: the work happens on your own hardware. Nothing is uploaded for processing, because there is no server to upload it to.

Local-first, by design

Most software in this category sends your files somewhere to be handled. HSX NanoCodify does not. Processing runs on your CPU or GPU, and your files stay in the folders you put them in. HSX NanoCodify does reach the internet for the content it displays, but nothing about you or your files travels the other way.

That is not a setting you enable. It is how the application is built, and it is why there is no account to create, no profile to build and no subscription to lapse. Full detail is in the HSX NanoCodify privacy policy.

Questions

Does HSX NanoCodify need an internet connection?

Yes, because HSX NanoCodify works with content that lives online. That connection is used to fetch that content and nothing else: your settings, your history and your files are held on your device and are never sent to Hasnain Studio X.

Does HSX NanoCodify require an account?

No. There is no registration, no sign-in and no online identity. You install the application and use it.

What data does HSX NanoCodify collect?

None. There is no analytics SDK, no usage tracking and no crash reporting that leaves your device. The full detail is in the HSX NanoCodify privacy policy.

Is HSX NanoCodify a subscription?

No. HSX NanoCodify is a free trial followed by a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There is no recurring fee.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
