  • No-code visual 2D game building

Who it is for

HSX GameFabrix is built for anyone who enjoys exploring simulated worlds without an always-on connection.

It sits in the games & explore part of the Hasnain Studio X catalogue, and it follows the same rule as every other title in the range: the work happens on your own hardware. Nothing is uploaded for processing, because there is no server to upload it to.

Local-first, by design

Most software in this category sends your files somewhere to be handled. HSX GameFabrix does not. Processing runs on your CPU or GPU, and your files stay in the folders you put them in. The application keeps working with the network switched off.

That is not a setting you enable. It is how the application is built, and it is why there is no account to create, no profile to build and no subscription to lapse. Full detail is in the HSX GameFabrix privacy policy.

Questions

Does HSX GameFabrix need an internet connection?

No. HSX GameFabrix does its work on your own device. You can install it, disconnect, and it keeps functioning. A connection is only used by the store itself, for installation and licence checks.

Does HSX GameFabrix require an account?

No. There is no registration, no sign-in and no online identity. You install the application and use it.

What data does HSX GameFabrix collect?

None. There is no analytics SDK, no usage tracking and no crash reporting that leaves your device. The full detail is in the HSX GameFabrix privacy policy.

Is HSX GameFabrix a subscription?

No. HSX GameFabrix is a free trial followed by a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There is no recurring fee.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
