What HSX FotoTensor does

  • Text-to-image generation running entirely on your hardware
  • Face-aware portrait enhancement and processing
  • Always-on content safety - explicit prompts blocked outright
  • Local library index with voluntary report-only feedback

Who it is for

HSX FotoTensor is built for creators who want generative tooling on their own GPU, with no API keys, no queue and nothing metered per use.

It sits in the AI tools part of the catalogue, and your photographs never leave the machine to be processed. There is no server behind HSX FotoTensor to send them to.

Local-first, by design

Plenty of the alternatives send your photographs to a server to be handled. HSX FotoTensor does not. Processing runs on your CPU or GPU, and the photographs you open stay where you put them. The application keeps working with the network switched off.

That is not a setting you enable, it is how HSX FotoTensor is built, which is why there is no account to create and no subscription to lapse. It does ask for a graphics card with at least 6 GB of dedicated memory, because the work it would otherwise send to a server happens on that card instead. Full detail is in the HSX FotoTensor privacy policy.

Questions

Does HSX FotoTensor need an internet connection?

No. HSX FotoTensor does its work on your own device. You can install it, disconnect, and it keeps functioning. A connection is only used by the store itself, for installation and licence checks.

Does HSX FotoTensor need a dedicated graphics card?

Yes. HSX FotoTensor runs its processing on your own GPU and needs at least 6 GB of dedicated graphics memory. Shared or integrated graphics below that will either refuse to start or fall back to a much slower path.

Does HSX FotoTensor require an account?

No. There is no registration, no sign-in and no online identity. You install the application and use it.

What data does HSX FotoTensor collect?

None. There is no analytics, no usage tracking and no crash reporting that leaves your device, and your photographs are never uploaded. The full detail is in the HSX FotoTensor privacy policy.

Is HSX FotoTensor a subscription?

No. HSX FotoTensor is a free trial followed by a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There is no recurring fee.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
