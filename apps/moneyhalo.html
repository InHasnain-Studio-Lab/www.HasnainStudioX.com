MoneyHalo

Watchlists, signals and market calendars in one calm dashboard

A market tracker for Windows. MoneyHalo keeps your watchlists, portfolio and alerts on your own PC while pulling live quotes, charts, earnings dates and IPO calendars from public market data providers.

What MoneyHalo does

  • Watchlists, portfolio and price alerts stored locally
  • Earnings centre, IPO radar and catalyst calendar
  • Profit and risk radar with signal history
  • Currency conversion and symbol detail charts

Who it is for

MoneyHalo is built for designers, writers and small studios who need professional output without a monthly bill.

It sits in the creative & documents part of the Hasnain Studio X catalogue, and it follows the same rule as every other title in the range: the work happens on your own hardware. Nothing is uploaded for processing, because there is no server to upload it to.

Local-first, by design

Most software in this category sends your files somewhere to be handled. MoneyHalo does not. Processing runs on your CPU or GPU, and your files stay in the folders you put them in. MoneyHalo does reach the internet for the content it displays, but nothing about you or your files travels the other way.

That is not a setting you enable. It is how the application is built, and it is why there is no account to create, no profile to build and no subscription to lapse. Full detail is in the MoneyHalo privacy policy.

Questions

Does MoneyHalo need an internet connection?

Yes, because MoneyHalo works with content that lives online. That connection is used to fetch that content and nothing else: your settings, your history and your files are held on your device and are never sent to Hasnain Studio X.

Does MoneyHalo require an account?

No. There is no registration, no sign-in and no online identity. You install the application and use it.

What data does MoneyHalo collect?

None. There is no analytics SDK, no usage tracking and no crash reporting that leaves your device. The full detail is in the MoneyHalo privacy policy.

Is MoneyHalo a subscription?

No. MoneyHalo is a free trial followed by a one-time purchase through the Microsoft Store. There is no recurring fee.

Which versions of Windows does it support?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
