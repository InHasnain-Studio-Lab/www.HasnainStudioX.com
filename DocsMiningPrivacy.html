Privacy Policy

DocsMining is a document scanner that does its work on your device. Your scans, the text recognised from them and anything you translate stay on the phone. There is no account, no sync and no server of ours to send them to.

Effective date: 17 Sep 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: DocsMining for Android

1. Overview

DocsMining is built local first. Scanning, text recognition, translation and handwriting recognition all run on your device.

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • We operate no server that receives your documents.
  • Your scans, page images and recognised text never leave the device.
  • We do not sell, share or monetise user data.

Your documents stay on your device, always.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

DocsMining does not collect, store or transmit any of the following:

Personal Identity

No name, email address, phone number, postal address or account identifier. DocsMining has no sign in.

Usage And Analytics

No usage analytics, no event tracking, no crash reporting SDK, no behavioural profiling. The app ships with no telemetry library of any kind.

Your Documents

Your scans, page images, recognised text, translations, annotations and handwriting are never uploaded, never sampled and never seen by us.

Sensitive System Data

No location, no contacts list, no call or message data, no microphone, no device fingerprinting.

We operate no servers that receive your content. The only outbound calls DocsMining makes are listed in section 4, and none of them carries a document.

3. Data Stored Locally On Your Device

App Settings

Your theme, accent, capture filter, export defaults and toggles, saved in the private storage of the app.

Your Documents

Page images, recognised text, translations, annotations, folders and the per document history, held in the private database and file storage of the app.

The Vault

Documents you move into the vault are encrypted on the device with AES-GCM. The key is held in the Android keystore, and vaulted text is removed from the search index while it is locked.

Language Models

Downloaded translation and handwriting language models, so those features keep working offline. You can delete them from Settings.

Licence State

Your Pro unlock flag and, when a rewarded window is running, its expiry. Stored in the device keystore. We receive nothing.

None of this leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it. Because there is no account and no cloud backup, uninstalling the app removes it all.

4. Network And Third Party Services

These are every outbound call DocsMining makes, and exactly what each one sends.

Purchase Validation

When you buy Pro, the purchase token, the product id and the package name of this app are sent to our validation endpoint, which asks Google whether the purchase is genuine. No document data is included, and no personal identifier is sent.

Language Model Downloads

The first time you translate into a language, or write in one on the handwriting pad, that language model is downloaded from Google. The request carries the language, nothing else. After that the feature works offline.

Google AdMob

In the free tier the app requests Google AdMob native ads. AdMob may use your Advertising ID for ad personalisation and frequency capping, and in the EEA and UK the app shows the Google consent form first. Your documents are never part of an ad request. Buying Pro removes all ads.

Fonts

The typeface of the app is fetched once from Google Fonts on first launch and cached locally afterwards.

Links And Sharing

Links to our website open in your browser. Sharing an export hands the file to the app you choose; from that point it is the policy of that app which applies.

5. Google Play Purchases

The one time Pro unlock is billed exclusively by Google Play.

  • We never process payment information.
  • We never store card or payment details.
  • No card data ever reaches the app or the developer.
  • We do not receive your Google account details.

The app receives only a purchase token, which it passes to our validator so Google can confirm the purchase is genuine.

6. App Permissions

Each permission DocsMining declares, what it powers and what it does not do.

Camera

Powers the document scanner. The camera is opened only while you are scanning. Nothing is recorded, and no photo is taken except the pages you capture.

Photos And Media

Lets you import pages you already have. The app reads only the images you pick; it never scans your gallery in the background.

Biometrics

Unlocks the vault using your fingerprint, face or device passcode. The app never sees your biometric data; Android performs the check and returns only a yes or no.

Contacts

Requested only when you tap "Save as contact" on a scanned business card, and used only to write that contact. Your contacts are never read for any other purpose and never leave the device.

Internet

Used for the four calls listed in section 4 and nothing else. The app is fully usable offline once your language models are downloaded.

Advertising ID

Used by Google AdMob in the free tier for ad personalisation and frequency capping. It is never linked to your documents. Pro removes ads, and this identifier is then unused.

7. Children and Privacy

DocsMining is a general purpose productivity tool. It is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no data from any user regardless of age.

8. Policy Updates

If this policy changes we will update the effective date and the app version it applies to. This page was last updated for DocsMining 1.5.0 on 17 September 2026. Any change will stay consistent with the core principle: your documents stay on your device.
