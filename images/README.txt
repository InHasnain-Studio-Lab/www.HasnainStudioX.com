PUT YOUR AI ARTWORK IN THIS FOLDER
==================================

1. Copy your image files in here (.jpg .png .webp).
2. Go up one folder and double-click  update-gallery.bat
3. Refresh the AI Studio page. Done.

NAMING (optional — makes the gallery look better)
-------------------------------------------------
Name files in three parts separated by DOUBLE underscores,
using hyphens instead of spaces:

    category__Title__How-it-was-made.jpg

Examples:
    portrait__Neon-Rain__StudioFlow-SDXL.jpg
    concept__Obsidian-City__Vanta-Local-GPU.png
    video__Motion-Study-01__Stillmotion.jpg

Categories that get their own filter button:
    portrait   concept   video   product
Anything else is grouped under "Other".

A plain name like  my-render.png  works fine too — it just
shows up as "My Render" with no subtitle.

You can hand-edit any title or description inside gallery-data.js
afterwards; re-running the tool keeps your edits.
