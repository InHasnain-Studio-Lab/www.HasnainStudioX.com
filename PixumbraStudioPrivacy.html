Privacy Policy

Pixumbra Studio is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Every picture you open is read, worked on and written on your own PC, and never leaves it.

Effective date: 22 August 2026  ·  Version: 1.0.0.0  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: Pixumbra Studio (Windows App)

1. Overview

Pixumbra Studio is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

Every instrument, including cutout, enlarge, portrait repair and clean, runs on your own machine. The app needs no internet connection to work on a picture.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Pixumbra Studio does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Your Content or Files

No picture you open, nothing the app writes out from it, and none of the recipes or credit lines you set up.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

Advertisement Ad space reserved — activates once AdSense review completes

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your ground, accent, engine choices, worker count, credit line and output folder are saved in a small settings file on your own PC.

Your Pictures and Exports

Pictures are read from wherever you opened them and written to the output folder you choose. The app keeps no copy of its own and transmits nothing.

Recipes and Fault Log

Saved recipes are plain text files beside the settings. If something goes wrong, the app writes a single local text file describing the fault so the problem can be looked at. Neither is ever transmitted.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

Pixumbra Studio is a paid app with a free Microsoft Store trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

Pixumbra Studio declares only internet access, used for the Store licence check, and the full trust desktop capability it needs to read and write picture files. Nothing else.

File Access

Only when you open a picture, choose a folder to run on, or save a result. The app never scans your drives in the background.

On-Device Processing

Cutting out subjects, enlarging, repairing faces, clearing speckle, grading, marking and converting all run on your own PC, on your processor or your graphics card. Your pictures are never sent anywhere to be processed.

Network Access

Used only for the Microsoft Store licence check. The app makes no other outbound connection, and needs no internet to work on a picture.

6. Children's Privacy

Pixumbra Studio is a general-purpose picture tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
