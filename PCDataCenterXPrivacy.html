Privacy Policy

PC DataCenterX is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Every drive reading and backup job runs on your PC.

Effective date: 13 July 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: PC DataCenterX (Windows App)

1. Overview

PC DataCenterX is a Windows storage and home-server dashboard built with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy. It reads drive health and capacity and runs backup jobs directly on your PC.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit your system information to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All drive readings, vault operations, and backup jobs happen locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

PC DataCenterX does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.

Your System Information

PC DataCenterX reads details such as drive capacity, SMART health, wear, temperature, BitLocker and TPM state only to show them to you on screen. This information is never transmitted anywhere.

Your Files and Backups

Vault contents, restore points, and backup copies stay on the drives you choose. They are never uploaded, indexed, or sent to us.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your adopted drives, theme, and dashboard preferences are saved locally under your user profile only.

Backup and Vault Data

Backup copies and vault restore points are written only to the drives and folders you select.

Capacity History

Usage readings used for capacity forecasting are stored locally so the projection reflects your own drives.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

PC DataCenterX is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Full Trust Desktop

PC DataCenterX runs as a full trust desktop app so it can perform real maintenance, such as clearing caches, resetting the network stack, and repairing system files. These actions run locally.

Administrator Approval

Repairs that change protected settings ask for Windows administrator approval each time, so you always stay in control.

On-Device Processing

Every scan, cleanup, and repair is performed on your PC. No system data is sent off the device.

Network Access

Used only for the Microsoft Store licence check and for network tools you start yourself, such as Ping, Traceroute, the DNS benchmark, and the connection speed test. Those tools contact the public servers being tested (for example your DNS provider or a public speed-test host), never us. No data is sent to Hasnain Studio X.

6. Children's Privacy

PC DataCenterX is a general-purpose utility tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
