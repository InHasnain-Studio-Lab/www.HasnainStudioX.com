Privacy Policy

HSX NovaDiffux is a local AI image‑creation workstation for Windows, developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Image creation, refinement and every finishing tool run on your own device.

Effective date: 6 August 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: HSX NovaDiffux for Windows, formerly HSX NovaStudio Pro (version 1.0.8.0)

1. Overview

HSX NovaDiffux runs entirely on your own PC and needs graphics with at least 4 GB of video memory. The image model ships inside the app, so it works with no internet connection and nothing to download.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not operate servers for this app.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.
  • Nothing you create is ever uploaded.

The only outbound internet use in the whole app is the Microsoft Store licence check.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

HSX NovaDiffux does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers. The app has no accounts and no sign-in.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no update pings, no advertising SDKs.

User Content

No prompts, images, face photos, model files, or anything else you create or load inside the app.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

There is no cloud processing. We do not run servers for HSX NovaDiffux, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your preferences and configuration are saved in the app's local app-data folder on your PC only.

Prompt History and Image Tags

Prompts you have used and tags you apply to images are stored locally so you can find your work again.

Saved Face Photos

Photos you choose for the face tool are stored locally in the app's app-data folder. They are never transmitted, and you can delete them at any time.

Local Log

A plain log file kept on your device to help you troubleshoot. It is never sent anywhere.

Add‑On Styles

Any add‑on style files you add yourself are read from your own folder and stay where you put them. They are never uploaded.

Created Images

Images you create are saved to your Pictures folder in a HSX NovaDiffux subfolder. Each image stores its own recipe - prompt, seed and size - inside the PNG file.

This data never leaves your device. The image engine runs as a program on your own PC and opens no network connection of any kind.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

HSX NovaDiffux is a paid app with a Store-managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment or card details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

The licence check is the only outbound internet call the app makes.

5. App Permissions

Internet Client

Used solely for the Microsoft Store licence check. There are no other outbound calls, no update pings, and no crash reporting.

Full-Trust Desktop

Required to run image creation on your GPU and to read the model files and photos you select.

File Access

Only for the files and folders you choose, plus the HSX NovaDiffux subfolder in your Pictures folder where created images are saved. No background scanning.

Responsible Use

Every generated image carries a visible AI mark, and a safety filter is always on and cannot be switched off.

6. Children's Privacy

HSX NovaDiffux is a general audience creative tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
