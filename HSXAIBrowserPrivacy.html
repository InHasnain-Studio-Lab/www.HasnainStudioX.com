Privacy Policy

HSX AI Browser is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. The model that summarises and rewrites runs on your own PC, and no page you read is ever uploaded.

Effective date: 9 September 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Studio X  ·  Developer: Hasnain Butt Akhtar  ·  Applies to: HSX AI Browser (Windows App)

1. Overview

HSX AI Browser is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All processing happens locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

HSX AI Browser does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

User Content

No prompts, text, images, files, or anything you create inside the app.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so nothing can be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your preferences and configuration are saved locally on your device only.

User-Created Content

Saved prompts, notes, or any content you create stays on your device and is never transmitted.

Local Cache

Temporary performance cache stored locally and never transmitted.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. The AI Features

Summaries, key point extraction, tone rewriting, drafting, page comparison and research mode all work the same way.

The Model Is On This PC

The model that reads a page and writes the summary is a file installed with the app on your own machine. It runs on your hardware.

Page Text Is Never Uploaded

The page you are reading, the passage you ask to be rewritten and the report research mode produces are handled entirely on this PC. None of it is sent to us or to anybody else.

No Account and No Key

There is nothing to sign in to and no API key to enter, because there is no external AI service involved.

Nothing Is Kept Afterwards

A summary exists while you are looking at it. Nothing you generate is retained, logged, or used to train anything.

5. What a Browser Necessarily Does

Honesty about the one thing a browser cannot avoid, and the protections that limit it.

Websites You Visit See You

Loading a page means your computer contacts that website, which learns your IP address the same as with any browser. That is how the web works and no browser can change it. It is a connection between you and that site; nothing passes through us.

Ad and Tracker Blocking

Requests to known advertising and tracking hosts are blocked before they are made, so those companies never hear from you at all.

Anti-Fingerprinting and Leak Protection

The signals a site reads to identify your machine are reduced, and the paths that commonly leak your real address around a connection are closed off.

HTTPS-Only Mode and Secure DNS

Connections are upgraded to encrypted ones where a site supports it, and address lookups are sent over an encrypted connection to the resolver set in Settings rather than in plain text across your network.

Your history, bookmarks, cookies, passwords and cache stay in your profile on this PC. There is no cloud sync, because there is no cloud.

6. Microsoft Store Licensing

HSX AI Browser may offer free trials, one-time purchases, or durable add-ons (lifetime unlocks). All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

7. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

HSX AI Browser requests only the minimum permissions required for core functionality. Permissions are used solely for app features and never for data collection.

File Access

Only when you choose a file, never background scanning.

Clipboard Access

Only when you paste or copy within the app.

Network Access

Used only for the features below, and even then, no data is sent to us. Address bar suggestions query DuckDuckGo's autocomplete with the text you type (switchable off in Settings). Site icons load directly from each site. The optional one-click Speed Test sends and receives test data with Cloudflare's public speed-test network, only while a test is running. The optional per-entry breach-check in Passwords sends a 5-character partial hash of a password (never the password itself) to the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords API, only when you click "Check for breaches." Every other feature runs entirely on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

HSX AI Browser is a general-purpose productivity/utility tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

9. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
