Find your problem

My PC takes ages to start

The free way

Open Settings, then Apps, then Startup, and switch off anything you do not need the moment Windows starts. Task Manager shows the same list.

Going further

PC TuneX. One-click junk cleanup, startup control and background process management on one screen, so you are not hunting through three places.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for PC TuneX

My drive keeps filling up

The free way

Open Settings, then System, then Storage. Cleanup recommendations and Storage Sense remove temporary files and empty the Recycle Bin.

Going further

PC TurboX Ultra. Adds a large-file finder, a duplicate scanner and a clean uninstaller, so you see what is actually using the space, not only temporary files.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for PC TurboX Ultra

I have the same photos saved over and over

The free way

Windows has no duplicate photo finder of its own. You can sort a folder by size and compare by eye, which works for a handful of pictures.

Going further

MediaTidy Ultra. Finds duplicates and near-duplicates in the folders you choose, compresses what is oversized, and can undo any run from its session log.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for MediaTidy Ultra

I need to move files between two PCs

The free way

Nearby sharing, in Settings under System, sends files between Windows PCs that are close together.

Going further

QuantumDrop. Drag and drop files between Windows PCs on the same Wi-Fi, with no cloud, no cables and no account.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for QuantumDrop

I want to zip files with a password

The free way

Windows can make and open zip files from File Explorer, but it cannot protect a zip with a password.

Going further

PC Archive Pro. Compresses, extracts and encrypts archives, checks file hashes and searches inside archives, all on your PC.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for PC Archive Pro

I need to convert a file to another format

The free way

Paint and Photos can save a picture in a few other formats. Windows has no built-in converter for video, audio or documents.

Going further

HSX ConvertStudio. Converts documents, images, video and audio between formats on your own PC, with nothing uploaded to a website converter.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX ConvertStudio

Big downloads keep failing halfway

The free way

Your browser can resume some interrupted downloads from its downloads list, depending on the site.

Going further

PC Download Manager Ultra. Splits downloads into segments, resumes them after a drop, checks the finished file and can extract archives for you.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for PC Download Manager Ultra

I am worried about losing my files

The free way

File History, in the Control Panel, keeps copies of your folders on another drive. OneDrive keeps version history for files you sync to it.

Going further

HSX FileGuardian Ultra. Keeps protected copies and earlier versions of the folders you choose on your own storage, with no cloud involved.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX FileGuardian Ultra

I want Windows to share less about me

The free way

Open Settings, then Privacy and security. Camera, microphone, location, diagnostics and the advertising ID each have their own page.

Going further

PC GuardX. Puts those switches on one dashboard, adds ad-domain blocking and encrypted DNS presets, and every change can be reversed.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for PC GuardX

I need a USB stick that can start my PC

The free way

Windows makes a recovery drive (search for Recovery Drive), and Microsoft offers the Media Creation Tool for Windows installation media.

Going further

HSX BootForge. Writes bootable USB drives from your own disk images and keeps a history of what you have written.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX BootForge

I need to edit PDFs and Office files without a subscription

The free way

Microsoft Edge opens PDFs and lets you highlight, draw and fill in forms. Office on the web is free but needs an account and a connection.

Going further

HSX WorkX Suite. Edits PDFs, writes documents and builds spreadsheets with formulas and charts on your PC, with a one-time purchase.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX WorkX Suite

I want help rewriting text without sending it online

The free way

Windows has no offline writing assistant. Notepad and WordPad style tools leave the rewriting to you.

Going further

HSX WriteDesk. Shortens, expands or changes the tone of text, and a shortcut rewrites the text you select in any app, all on your own PC.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX WriteDesk

I need to translate a document offline

The free way

Windows has no offline document translator. Online translators need you to upload the document.

Going further

HSX DocMento. Reads, detects the language of and translates documents on your PC, including scanned pages, and nothing leaves the machine.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX DocMento

I want to record my screen for a tutorial

The free way

Snipping Tool records the screen on Windows 11, and the Xbox Game Bar (Windows key and G) records a single app window.

Going further

PC Screen Recorder Pro. Records screen and sound with chapters and markers, grabs stills, and turns the recording into an edited video or a written step guide.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for PC Screen Recorder Pro

I want to edit a video without uploading it

The free way

Clipchamp comes with Windows 11 and needs you to sign in with a Microsoft account.

Going further

HSX PrimeCut Ultra. Imports, edits, colour grades and exports video entirely on your PC, with no account and no upload.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX PrimeCut Ultra

My photos need more than a quick fix

The free way

The Photos app crops, straightens and adjusts light and colour.

Going further

HSX GlowCraft. A full photo editor with enhancement tools that run on your own PC, so your photos are never uploaded.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX GlowCraft

I want to show my screen to someone in the room

The free way

Press the Windows key and K to cast to a TV or display that supports Miracast.

Going further

HSX CastVisuality. A viewer scans a QR code and your screen opens in their browser, with no app to install. Share one window or a whole monitor, and hand over mouse and keyboard when you want to.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX CastVisuality

Games and films sound flat on my headphones

The free way

Windows Sonic for Headphones, under Sound settings and spatial sound, adds a basic surround effect.

Going further

SpatiaX Ultra. Real-time 3D spatial sound for headphones and speakers, with signature profiles, running on your PC.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for SpatiaX Ultra

I want to tune the sound of every app at once

The free way

Windows has no system-wide equaliser. Some sound cards ship their own control panel.

Going further

Hypersonus Ultra. Applies surround, 3D modes and a ten-band equaliser to every app at the same time, with a profile per output device.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for Hypersonus Ultra

My eyes are tired after a long day at the screen

The free way

Night light, in Settings under System and Display, warms the colours in the evening.

Going further

PC VisionBulwark. Adjusts colour temperature and brightness curves from one dashboard, with more control than a single warmth slider.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for PC VisionBulwark

I keep doing the same steps every day

The free way

Power Automate for desktop is free on Windows and needs you to sign in.

Going further

HSX Automafy. Builds repeatable flows with variables and reusable steps that run on your PC, with no account and no cloud sync.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX Automafy

I need to repeat the same clicks over and over

The free way

Windows has no built-in auto-clicker.

Going further

HSX SmartClicker. Sets click intervals and patterns, records macros and hotkeys, and keeps every profile on your PC.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX SmartClicker

I get distracted when I should be working

The free way

Focus sessions, in the Clock app on Windows 11, time your work and silence notifications.

Going further

HSX FocusRoom Ultra. A focus workspace with timed sessions and your own statistics, kept entirely on your device.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX FocusRoom Ultra

I want to control my PC by voice

The free way

Voice access on Windows 11 lets you open apps, click and dictate by speaking.

Going further

HSX HandsFree PC. Adds custom phrases, multi-step voice routines and per-app commands, with speech recognised on your PC.

Free trial on Microsoft Store for HSX HandsFree PC

My Android phone is out of space

The free way

Open Settings, then Storage, to see what is using space and clear app caches one by one.

Going further

Mobile TuneX. Shows storage by real size, clears only the cache and junk you tick, and finds duplicate and blurry photos with a preview before every deletion.

Get it on Google Play for Mobile TuneX

I need to convert or shrink a video on my phone

The free way

Android has no built-in converter. Sharing apps often shrink videos, at a quality you do not choose.

Going further

ConvertMaster Ultra. Fifty tools that convert video, extract audio, compress images and apply one-tap social presets, all on the phone with no upload.

Get it on Google Play for ConvertMaster Ultra

I need to turn paper into text I can search on my phone

The free way

Android can copy text from a photo in many camera and gallery apps, a little at a time.

Going further

DocsMining. Finds the page edges, straightens and cleans the scan, recognises the text and translates it, all on the phone with the radio off.

Get it on Google Play for DocsMining
