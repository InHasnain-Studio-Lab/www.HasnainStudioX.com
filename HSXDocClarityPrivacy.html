Privacy Policy

HSX DocClarity is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy. Your documents are read entirely on your own device.

Effective date: 1 June 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: HSX DocClarity (Windows App)

1. Overview

HSX DocClarity is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy. Document analysis runs on an on-device model on your own PC.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not transmit your documents to external servers.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All document processing happens locally on your device.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

HSX DocClarity does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Your Documents or Content

No document text, files, questions, or results you create inside the app.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location.

We do not run servers, so your documents cannot be uploaded even by accident.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your theme, accent, and preferences are saved locally on your device only.

Your Documents and Results

Documents you import and any analysis or exports you create stay on your device and are never transmitted.

On-Device Model

The language model used for analysis runs locally. Bundled or user-provided model files stay on your device.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

HSX DocClarity is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

HSX DocClarity requests only the minimum permissions required for core functionality. Permissions are used solely for app features and never for data collection.

File Access

Only the documents you choose to import - never background scanning.

On-Device Processing

Analysis is performed locally by a model running on your PC. Your document text is never sent anywhere.

Network Access

Used only for the Microsoft Store licence check. No document data is sent to us or anyone else.

6. Children's Privacy

HSX DocClarity is a general-purpose productivity tool. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your documents stay on your device.
