SpatiaX Ultra Mobile

Real-time 3D spatial audio for Android

The Android version of SpatiaX Ultra - a real-time audio engine that transforms your headphone or speaker experience with 3D spatial sound and pro-grade DSP, running entirely on device.

  • 3D spatial surround for headphones and speakers
  • Profiles for gaming, music, movies, and calls

Creative & Documents

Design, publishing, web and office work

DocsMining

Extract and analyse content from documents

A local-first document intelligence tool for Android - extract text, tables, and key data from PDFs, images, and scanned documents, all processed privately on your device.

  • Extract text and tables from PDFs and scanned documents
  • OCR support for images and handwritten notes
  • Export results as plain text, CSV, or structured JSON
  • Fully offline - no cloud upload, no server processing

WorkX Suite

PDF, documents and maths in one Android suite

The Android edition of WorkX Suite - three modules (XPDF, XDOC and XMATH) plus an on-device AI Copilot. Everything you open or create stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself.

  • XPDF, XDOC and XMATH modules in one app
  • On-device AI Copilot with your own optional API key
  • Biometric lock backed by the Android Keystore
  • No account, no sign-in, no third-party analytics SDK

XCipher In development

Local file and text encryption on Android

A local-first file and text encryption tool for Android. Encrypt files, folders, and clipboard content using AES-256 - everything stays on your device, nothing leaves.

  • AES-256 file and folder encryption
  • Clipboard and text snippet encryption
  • Drag-and-drop interface with instant feedback
  • Completely offline - no keys sent anywhere

FlipX Studio In development

Convert audio, video, and images on Android

The Android edition of FlipX Studio - a local-first media toolkit for fast, high-quality conversion of audio, video, and image files, processed entirely on your device.

  • Convert audio, video, and image files locally
  • Smart output presets for quality and file size
  • Batch processing with progress tracking
  • No upload required - all processing stays on device

ConvertMaster Ultra

Convert and compress media on your Android device

ConvertMaster Ultra converts and compresses video, audio, image and PDF files entirely on your Android device using an on-device media engine. Files you pick are read locally and results are written locally - nothing is uploaded.

  • Video, audio, image and PDF conversion on-device
  • Compression presets for quality and file size
  • Job queue with adjustable concurrency and a local library
  • No analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK, no sign-in
