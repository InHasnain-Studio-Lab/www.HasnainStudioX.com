Privacy Policy

PlanetX: Infinity is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local‑first, privacy‑first design philosophy.

Effective date: 7 July 2026  ·  Publisher: Hasnain Butt Akhtar / Hasnain Studio X  ·  Applies to: PlanetX: Infinity (Windows App)

1. Overview

PlanetX: Infinity is developed by Hasnain Studio X with a local-first, privacy-first design philosophy.

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not require an account and there are no cloud saves.
  • We do not operate servers for our apps.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize user data.

All flying, rendering, and progression happens locally on your device. The only network use is the optional in-game Earth weather layer (anonymous map coordinates sent to a public weather service) and the Microsoft Store licence check.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

PlanetX: Infinity does not collect, store, or send any of the following:

Personal Identity Data

No name, email, phone number, address, or identifiers. Your pilot callsign stays on your PC.

Usage Data or Analytics

No telemetry, crash analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

User Content

No flight saves, photos, journal entries, or anything you create inside the game.

Sensitive System Data

No passwords, tokens, credentials, browsing history, or system logs.

Location Data

No GPS, IP-based location, or device location - the app never requests any location permission. The optional Home Beacon uses coordinates you type in yourself, saved locally and never transmitted. Weather lookups use the coordinates of your in-game spaceship, not of you.

We do not run servers. The app talks only to the Microsoft Store and a public weather service - never to us.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

App Settings

Your preferences, pilot profile, configuration, and any home coordinates you choose to type in are saved locally on your device only.

Flight Progress and Photos

Saves, missions, credits, medals, the discovery journal, and photo-mode shots (saved to your Pictures folder) stay on your device and are never transmitted.

Local Cache

A small weather cache keeps the Earth live layer working offline. Stored locally and never transmitted.

Local Trial or Licence State

Managed entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app reads it locally; we receive nothing.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

4. Microsoft Store Licensing

PlanetX: Infinity is a paid app with a Microsoft Store managed free trial. All licensing, payments, and transactions are handled exclusively by Microsoft.

  • We do not process payment information.
  • We do not store payment details.
  • We do not receive your Microsoft account information.

5. App Permissions

Minimum Required Only

PlanetX: Infinity requests only the minimum permissions required for core functionality. Permissions are used solely for app features and never for data collection.

File Access

Photo mode writes PNG images to your own Pictures folder - never background scanning.

On-Device Processing

The whole solar system - orbits, rendering, physics, voice callouts, and progression - is computed locally on your PC.

Network Access

Used only for the live Earth weather layer (anonymous coordinates of the in-game ship, or a place name you type, sent to a public weather service - no account, no identifiers) and the Microsoft Store licence check. No data is sent to us.

6. Children's Privacy

PlanetX: Infinity is a general-audience space flight game. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect any data from any user.

7. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the version and effective date. Any changes will remain consistent with our core principle: Your data stays on your device.
